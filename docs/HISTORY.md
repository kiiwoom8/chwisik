## Owner preferences and past corrections — read before changing anything

The owner's settled choices, and the mistakes already made and corrected. Don't undo these or repeat them. When the owner corrects or reverses something, add it here (what was wrong → what is right) in the same commit as the fix, and add the usual dated entry below.

**출근 tab (근무 기록)**
- **One row per shift.** A night worker's two shifts on one day (last night's end + tonight's start) are **two separate rows**. (They were merged into one row per person per day on the owner's request; the owner later called that a wrong decision and asked to split them again. Don't merge them.)
- Order inside a day, from the **bottom**: **야간 (`00:00 –`, carried from last night) → 오전 → 오후 → 야간 (tonight's start)**, i.e. latest start on top, a carried-over shift counting as 00:00. (Was 야간 at the bottom, then 야간 on top inside merged rows; this is current.)
- Tonight's row ends at **`24:00`** when the rest has its own `00:00 –` row on the next day, never `00:00`, and never the next-day end (`22:30 – 08:00` above that person's `00:00 – 08:00` was confusing). Only a shift on the month's last day, whose rest isn't shown, keeps `22:30 – 08:00 (+1)`.
- Length labels for a night shift: before midnight (tonight's row, on top) **`당일 N`**; after midnight (the carried-over `00:00 –` row at the bottom of the next day) **`전날 N`**: it is 전날's 야간. (It was `다음날 N` while the two parts shared one row; with separate rows the owner pointed out it should be `전날`.) A shift still running after midnight is **`근무 중 · 전날 N`** on today's `00:00 –` row (time since 00:00). (Was `근무 중 · 다음날 N`, then `근무 중 · 당일 N` while rows were merged; with separate rows the owner said `전날` is correct.) Yesterday's part of it shows as finished (`22:30 – 24:00`), not 근무 중.
- Use **`당일`**, not `이날`, in page text.
- **[출근] / [퇴근] times are in 30-min slots** (owner: the store rarely counts 1–29 min). Rounded to the nearest slot, snapped to the other worker's 퇴근 / 출근 when it's within 30 min (09:13 → 09:00; 09:18 → 09:00 when the last 퇴근 was 09:00), and pressing 출근 closes a worker who forgot 퇴근 (`자동 퇴근`); their own 퇴근 press within 30 min doesn't move it. The owner said "within 10 min"; the 30-min window covers that and matches the 30-min counting. Hand entry is not rounded.
- The add link is **`+ 근무 직접 입력`** (no `(깜빡한 날)`).
